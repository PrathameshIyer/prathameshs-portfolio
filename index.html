01 About Me

Built to make
ideas clearer.

Research first. Clean writing second. Strategy throughout.

I’m Prathamesh, a content writer and strategist. I write blogs, website copy and brand content that make complicated subjects easier to follow without sanding off what makes them interesting.

My work spans e-commerce, education, fintech, technology and business. I care about good research, a clear point of view and writing that sounds like a person, not a content machine.

My sweet spot is the space between strategy and the sentence itself: figuring out what the reader needs, what the brand should say, and how to say it without overcomplicating things.

I also work on website copy and content direction when the words need to do more than simply fill a page.
